harmonised across waves. Columns of a SPACE_MICRO address: REF_AREA (the respondent's country,
column COUNTRY), PURPOSE (the online-purchase purpose code of a diary slot), INSTRUMENT (the
payment-method code of that slot), MEASURE, TIME_PERIOD.
  SPACE_2019_respondents.csv   weight GrossWeight; slots QB_2_n (purpose), QB_3_n (amount), QB_4_n (instrument), capped at QB_2T
  SPACE_2022_respondents.csv   weight Gross_weight; slots QB1_n (purpose), QB3_n (amount), QB4_n (instrument)
  SPACE_2024_respondents.csv   weight Gross_weight; slots QB1_n (purpose), QB3_n (amount), QB4_n (instrument)
  codebooks: SPACE 2024 Codebook.xlsx, SPACE 2022 Codebook.xlsx, SPACE variables definitions wide file.xlsx
MEASURE: PN_W weighted number of purchases; PN_U unweighted respondents-slots; PN_W_AMT weighted
purchases carrying a stated amount; EUR_W weighted euro value of stated amounts; EUR_MEDIAN the
lower weighted median of stated amounts (at least 30 stated amounts, else INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE).
Amounts 999997/999998/999999 are sentinels, not amounts. A purchase with no stated amount still
counts in PN_W. Survey counts are in UNITS, not millions.
