ATTRIBUTION — WHO PUBLISHED THE DATA IN THIS PACKET, AND ON WHAT TERMS IT IS HERE
====================================================================================================

Every data file under `ecb/` and `space/` was published by the EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK. They are
redistributed here UNMODIFIED, for evaluation use, with attribution to the European Central
Bank. Each file's md5 is listed in section 3: the bytes in this packet are the bytes as
published.

The ECB is the source of the data. It has not reviewed, approved or endorsed this packet, the
questions asked of it, or any result anyone derives from it.

## 4. THE TERMS THESE FILES ARE REUSED UNDER

   The ESCB's "Policy regarding the reuse of ESCB statistics":
   https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html

   The policy, quoted:

     "All publicly available ESCB statistics may be reused free of charge on the condition
      that the source is quoted (e.g. "Source: ECB statistics.") and that the statistics
      (including metadata) are not modified."

   Reuse is in accordance with the respective disclaimers issued by the ECB, and the policy
   notes that statistics may be changed as a result of revisions or other updates.

   ★THIS PACKET MEETS BOTH CONDITIONS, AND HERE IS WHERE EACH ONE IS MET:

     the source is quoted        "Source: ECB statistics." is the first line of this file
                                 and the first line of landscape/README.txt.

     nothing is modified         Every one of the nineteen files is byte-for-byte the file
                                 the ECB published, and section 3 publishes each one's md5 so
                                 that anyone can check it. ★This includes the METADATA the
                                 policy names: the three SPACE codebooks carry Excel's own
                                 `x15ac:absPath` entries, and they are left exactly as the ECB
                                 saved them. Stripping them would modify the file.

     the snapshot is dated       The ECB revises its statistics. These files are the snapshot
                                 of 10 July 2026 and are named and dated as such; a figure
                                 derived from them is a figure from that snapshot, not a claim
                                 about what the ECB publishes today.
