# ENV0 CHAIN LAYER — DELIVERY README

**RUN PROTOCOL: one chain paper per sandbox, delivered on its own with the data and the published
menu. No station paper is in reach, and no other chain paper is in reach. A score produced under
any other delivery is not a score of this environment.**

The 3 papers ship in **one packet** (ruled 1 September 2026). The founder may prefer a packet
per span; absent that word, one packet.

## The 3 spans

| id | starts | what the candidate is handed | scored assembly cells | rejections |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `full-chain-from-T1` | T1 | the brief, the data folder and the Kinds block | **25** | asked on this span |
| `mid-start-from-T3` | T3 | the structure, the source map and the Kinds block | **25** | — |
| `late-start-from-T6` | T6 | the extracted and completed figures per country | **25** | — |

★Only `full-chain-from-T1` is called an end-to-end, anywhere — in the ids, the reports, a deck or a
buyer conversation. The others are the mid start and the late start. Presented by
span, never lumped, and never merged with the relay's mean.

★**THE END-TO-END IS SCORED ON THE CLIENT'S NUMBER.** `full-chain-from-T1` asks for `market_value`
and nothing else: five cells, one per country. Its key still carries every other cell with its
value, marked as not asked, so nothing is lost and nothing unasked is paid. CALC is reported as
— on that span, because both of its relations need figures the page no longer asks the candidate
to commit; SOURCE is unchanged. The other two spans are unchanged at 25 cells.

## ⚠️ THE PACKET EXPOSURE, DISCLOSED

| paper | blind floor | **packet floor** |
|---|---|---|
| `full-chain-from-T1` | 0.0000 | **0.4000** |
| `mid-start-from-T3` | 0.0000 | **0.3600** |
| `late-start-from-T6` | 0.3600 | **0.3600** |

**The spans NEST.** The late start's GIVEN is material the deeper spans must produce, so a reader
holding every paper answers cells on the deeper papers from the shallowest paper's handout. This is
a property of the nested GIVENs the design predicted, measured rather than estimated, and declared
here exactly as the relay declares its own.

**It is closed by delivery, not by the papers.** `chain_delivery_env0.sandbox_conformance` **raises**
— it does not log — on a relay paper in a chain sandbox and on a chain paper in a relay
conversation. The measurement protocol never hands a candidate more than its own bundle.

★The blind floor is 0.0000 on every span that is not handed its figures, because the scored set is
*defined* as what the page does not reach. That is the design working, not a null result.

## What each paper reports

1. **Assembly correctness** — the figures per country against the key.
2. **SOURCE** — the declared route's addresses against the key's pins. Reported as — on a span that is handed its figures.
3. **Consistency (CALC)** — the candidate recomputed against its own inputs and its own declared choices.
4. **Rejections** — on the spans whose key carries them.
5. **`OUTPUT_within_5pct`** — a DIAGNOSTIC count, never combined with any of the above.

Never averaged into one number, and never into the relay's mean.

## The served data landscape — SHIPS BESIDE THIS PACKET

The data landscape is a **served surface**: the candidate reads `landscape/README.txt` and it states
conventions. It is not inside this packet — the harness serves it — so its bytes are NAMED here and
in `MD5SUMS.txt`, and the archive travels with the release. **A run taken against a landscape whose
README is not the md5 below is not a run of this release.**

| what | md5 |
|---|---|
| `landscape/README.txt` (164109 bytes) | `75bfafc2e91e3b77abe3f6c6d5ef4850` |
| `landscape/codelists/PROVENANCE.json` | `fc9c82b9d4fb5972f7b1d1e8f6f90c43` |

The README's value-by-value section is GENERATED by `landscape_readme_env0.py` from 31 ECB codelists
archived under `landscape/codelists/`, fetched 2026-09-16T06:25:54+00:00. `PROVENANCE.json` carries, per codelist, the URL it was
fetched from, the fetch time, the byte count, the file md5 **and a content md5 over the sorted
(code, name) pairs** — the file md5 is not reproducible from the endpoint, because the SDMX envelope
carries a per-request id and timestamp, so the content md5 is the one that survives a re-fetch and
the one an independent archive is diffed against.
